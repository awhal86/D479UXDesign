Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to pack power outlet adapters?

We use the same 120-volt outlets that are used in the U.S.

When can I buy alcohol?

Alcohol is not allowed to be sold between midnight and 9:00 a.m. You may drink responsibly from 9:01 a.m. to 11:59 p.m.

What is the legal drinking age?

The drinking age on Taniti is 18, though it is not strictly enforced.

What if I only speak English?

Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English, but in rural areas, especially among older residents, English is less common.

What if I need medical care?

Taniti has one hospital and several clinics. Many staff members speak multiple languages.

Is crime common in Taniti?

Violent crime is rare, but as tourism increases, pickpocketing and petty theft have become more common.

Are attractions open on holidays?

Taniti observes many national holidays. Tourist attractions and restaurants may close, so plan accordingly.

Do I need to exchange currency?

Taniti uses the U.S. dollar. Some businesses also accept euros and yen. Credit cards are widely accepted.
